The results for “business translation services” skew toward certified document translation and language agencies. Exbabel translates live speech, so the title deliberately adds “for live meetings,” and the FAQ explicitly distinguishes the service from certified document translation.

The event page combines “live event translation” with “conference interpretation” because those terms connect to the product's live audio and caption workflow. The page explains the distinction between AI translation and a qualified human interpreter.

Selected titles:

- **Events:** Live Event Translation & Conference Interpretation | Exbabel
- **Business:** Business Translation Services for Live Meetings | Exbabel

Both pages follow the church translation page's SEO pattern: unique metadata and canonical URL, one descriptive H1, useful H2 sections, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data, SoftwareApplication structured data, internal navigation, and sitemap entries. The copy is written for the page's actual use case rather than repeating keywords.
