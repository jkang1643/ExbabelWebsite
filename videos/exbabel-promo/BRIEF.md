# Exbabel - One congregation. Understood.

## Subject

Exbabel is a real-time translation platform for churches. It lets congregation members scan a QR code, choose their language, and hear the sermon translated live through their phone with audio and captions. The same system works for livestreams. The product requires no changes to the existing audio setup.

## Audience

Church leaders, pastors, and ministry technology coordinators evaluating tools to serve multilingual congregations.

## Tone

Warm, conversational, confident. Not a hard sell. The viewer should feel like a trusted colleague walking them through something useful.

## Voiceover

ElevenLabs v3 model. Warm, conversational male voice at 140-150 WPM. Leave 0.8s silent gaps at each [First Name] and [Church Name] for programmatic audio splicing. Strategic pauses after: That is pretty much it / The same thing works online / the three setup verbs.

## Music

Free BGM from HeyGen music library. Restrained, lyric-free, 88-104 BPM.
